Our Humanitarian Mission

A better-coordinated response can make a difference.

During natural disasters and humanitarian crises, fragmented communication and delayed dispatch cost precious lives. ResQ was built to unite emergency call intake, urgency triage, responder fleet coordination, inventory distribution, and road safety navigation into one cohesive operational platform.

By pairing real-time incident reports with live network accessibility and supply auditing, emergency operators ensure that every affected community receives the right help at the earliest possible moment.

  • Instant incident registration & urgency scoring
  • Automated responder team matching by skill and location
  • Live road accessibility tracking & shortest transit calculation
  • Fair relief inventory distribution with shortage alerts

Lifecycle of an Operation

How the Response Works

From the moment an emergency call is received to the successful demobilization of rescue teams, ResQ maintains an end-to-end coordinated workflow.

STEP 01

Report a Disaster

Citizens and field observers log incidents detailing location, disaster category, estimated casualties, and immediate rescue needs.

STEP 02

Assess & Prioritize

Emergencies are ranked by composite urgency scores factoring in severity, casualties, and elapsed wait time to address critical threats first.

STEP 03

Identify Suitable Team

ResQ matches incidents with qualified available rescue responders possessing the exact required tools (medical, boats, extrication).

STEP 04

Find a Viable Route

The system computes the safest, fastest transit path across the road network, automatically detouring around blocked roads and flood zones.

STEP 05

Allocate Resources

Critical relief supplies—such as trauma kits, food rations, clean water, and rescue craft—are allocated without risking warehouse shortages.

STEP 06

Track to Completion

Dispatchers monitor missions live. Upon mission completion, responders and reusable gear are returned to available status for new calls.

ℹ️ Composite Urgency Scoring Formula

Emergency urgency is calculated using: Score = (Severity Base Weight) + (Immediate Rescue Count × 15) + (Affected People × 5) + (Waiting Time in Minutes × 2). Critical emergencies are dispatched first; ties are settled by arrival sequence.
